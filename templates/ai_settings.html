5 What gets sent

Each feature sends only what it needs, and only when it runs:

FeatureWhat is sent
Sorting purchasesThe merchant name from a card transaction (for example "JOES CAFE"), with the list of categories. No amounts, dates or account details.
Spending tipsYour card spending totals by category for recent months and your top 20 merchants with totals.
Advisor reportsA summary of your finances: account balances, budget and spending by category, recurring bills and income, debts, investments, net worth, and the household profile if you set one up. No account numbers or passwords.

Each company's handling of this data is described in the step 2 instructions above. Nothing is sent for features you leave unticked.

With Ollama the data goes only to the Ollama server address you entered, which you run yourself.

Saving sends one tiny test message to check the key works.
Questions

How much will this cost? With the cheaper models, usually a few cents a month for a household. With the most capable models, roughly $1–3 a month. Set a spending limit with your provider (step 2 shows where) so a bill can never surprise you.

Is my API key safe? It's stored encrypted in this app's database, using the app's secret key, and it's left out of the backups you download from Admin. The page never shows it again. Anyone who can open this app can use the AI features, but cannot read the key. You can delete it any time ("Turn off and delete the saved key"). If a key ever leaks, delete it on the provider's website and make a new one.

Do these companies train their AI on my data? See the privacy note in step 2 for the service you picked. In short: Anthropic and OpenAI don't train on data sent with an API key by default. Google may use data sent on Gemini's free tier; turn on billing in Google AI Studio to avoid that.

Can I switch services later? Yes. Pick another one, paste its key and save. The old key is not reused for the new service.

How do I turn AI off? Click "Turn AI off" at the top of this page, or choose "Off" in step 1 and save.
