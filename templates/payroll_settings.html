Notes on Household Employer Rules

  • State rules vary. Check whether your state requires income tax withholding for household employees, and when state unemployment (SUTA) registration kicks in (many states use a $1,000-per-quarter trigger).
  • New household employers usually start on the state's default "new employer" SUTA rate; the state agency assigns your actual rate after registration.
  • Federal FICA applies once annual cash wages reach the annual threshold (2026: ~$2,900 — verify with IRS Pub 926). It then covers all of that year's cash wages, so the pay run that crosses the threshold also catches up FICA on the earlier runs.
  • FUTA applies if you pay $1,000+ in any quarter; only on the first $7,000 per employee.
  • Reporting: W-2 to employee by Jan 31; W-3 + Schedule H filed with your 1040.
