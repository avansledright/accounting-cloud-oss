Use the paid tier for your finances. Gemini has a free tier, but on it Google may use what you send to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. Google itself says not to send personal information to the free tier. Paying (a $5 minimum prepayment) switches that off.
  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account. Accept the terms. Google AI Studio creates a project and an API key for you automatically.
  2. Turn on billing (recommended, see above): on the API keys page, find your project's Billing tier column and click Set up billing. Enter your details and prepay at least $5. Leave auto-reload off for a hard cap.
  3. If no key is listed, click Create API key and choose your project.
  4. Click the key to copy it. New keys start with AQ. (older ones with AIza). Paste it into step 3 below.
  5. Optional: at aistudio.google.com/spend, Edit spend cap sets a monthly limit. Prepaid credit is also a hard limit by itself.

Privacy (paid tier): Google doesn't use your prompts or replies to improve its products, and keeps them only for a limited time to detect abuse. Gemini API terms.
