Welcome

Accounting Cloud keeps a ledger of your main checking account and projects its balance 12 months ahead from your recurring income and bills. This wizard sets up the basics in about five minutes: one account, your pay, and your regular bills. Everything can be changed later.

Shown on your dashboard. Stays in your own database.
"Today" and schedules use the server's time zone. Change it with TZ in .env (e.g. TZ=America/New_York) and restart.

What to do next

  • Open the ledger: see the 12-month projection, then mark projected items as paid (or record what actually happened) as the month goes on.
  • Set budget targets for day-to-day categories such as groceries and dining.
  • Import a credit card statement (CSV) to see spending by category.
  • Add investment holdings for live prices, dividends and projections.
  • Take a net worth snapshot; repeat monthly to build the history chart.
  • Connect an AI assistant with the MCP server: see docs/mcp-server.md.

You can walk through this wizard again from Admin. A getting-started checklist on the dashboard tracks the rest.
